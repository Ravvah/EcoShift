# EcoShift - Decision Science Lab

![alt text](image.png)

---

EcoShift is an experimental decision-making lab designed to anticipate and schedule flexible industrial loads. Its applied objective is to minimize electricity costs (€) and carbon emissions ($\text{CO}_2$) under operational constraints, dealing with french electricity price and co2 intensity evolution during the day.

In parallel, the project serves as a training to implement end-to-end Predictive & Optimization Systems.

---

## Goal & Core Problems 

EcoShift evolves through distinct methodological phases, each targeting a specific decision-theoretic paradigm:

### **Version 1 - Classical Predict-then-Optimize (Current Scope)**

**Goal:** Quantify downstream **Decision Regret** under single point-forecast error :

- Ignore uncertainty of single point forecasting 
- Identify business-aligned predictive metrics
- Optimization problem as a linear program 
- Measure decision degradation and solver stability under perturbations, data shifts.
